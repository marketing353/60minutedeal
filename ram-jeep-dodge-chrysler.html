Straight answers

The questions worth asking.

What exactly counts as a “dealer add-on”?+

Anything added by the dealership rather than the manufacturer, charged on top of the vehicle price: paint and fabric protection, nitrogen in the tyres, VIN etching, pinstripes, door guards, theft-protection packages, and “market adjustment”. We don't add them. Tax, title, licence and documentation fees are different — those are set by law and we show them as separate lines.

How do I know the vehicle is actually there?+

Because we quote from what we have, not from an advert. Cars do sell while people are deciding — if that happens we tell you before you set off, and we tell you what else genuinely fits instead. What we won't do is get you through the door and then start steering you somewhere more expensive.

Does the form affect my credit?+

No. Nothing on this form runs a credit check of any kind. The credit questions are estimates so we can point you at realistic numbers. An actual finance application is a separate step that needs your explicit authorisation.

Can I still trade in if I owe money on my car?+

Yes, and it's extremely common. Tell us on the form and we'll factor the outstanding balance into the figures rather than springing it on you later. If you owe more than it's worth, we'll say so plainly and show you what that means.

Will someone start phoning me?+

Not until you ask. You get your numbers by email first. If you'd rather talk it through, say so and we'll call — otherwise we won't.
